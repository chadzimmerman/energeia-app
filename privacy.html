Energeia – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 23, 2026  |  Last Updated: March 23, 2026

1. Overview

Energeia ("we," "our," or "the app") is a habit-tracking role-playing game developed by Chad Zimmerman. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the app experience:

  • Account Information: A username and password you create when setting up your character. These are stored securely and used only to identify your account within the app.
  • Habit & Progress Data: The habits you create, your completion history, streaks, character level, inventory items, and other in-game progress. This data is tied to your account so your journey is preserved across sessions.
  • Anonymous User ID: An automatically generated anonymous identifier used internally to associate your data with your account. This is not linked to any external identity.
  • Device Information: Basic device and operating system information may be collected automatically by our infrastructure provider (Supabase) for security and service operation purposes.

We do not collect:

  • Your real name, email address, or phone number (unless you choose to provide one for support)
  • Location data
  • Payment card information (all payments are handled entirely by Apple)
  • Health or biometric data
  • Contacts or calendar access

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Provide and maintain your in-game account and progress
  • Sync your data across sessions and devices
  • Respond to support requests if you contact us
  • Improve app stability and performance

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database platform that implements industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit (TLS/SSL). Row-level security policies ensure that each user can only access their own data.

Supabase's privacy policy can be found at supabase.com/privacy.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Energeia offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions (such as premium content or subscriber-exclusive features) through the Apple App Store.

  • All payment transactions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment card details or billing information.
  • Apple's handling of payment data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • You may restore previous purchases at any time using the "Restore Purchases" option in the app's Settings screen.
  • Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID account settings.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Supabase – database, authentication, and storage. (Privacy Policy)
  • Apple App Store – app distribution and payment processing. (Privacy Policy)

We do not integrate any advertising networks, analytics trackers (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel), or social media SDKs.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and progress data for as long as your account is active. You may permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time using the "Delete Account" option in the app's Settings screen. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

8. Children's Privacy

Energeia is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (available directly in-app)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
